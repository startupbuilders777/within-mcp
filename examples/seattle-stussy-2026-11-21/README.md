# Where is the Stüssy Archive in Seattle?

The Stüssy Archive opens at **1526 Bellevue Ave** on Friday, November 27, 2026. It runs through Sunday, December 6. Stüssy’s November 20 flyer gives the dates and address. Check the notice for an opening hour before making the trip. [Read Stüssy’s announcement](https://www.stussy.com/blogs/news/stussy-archive-seattle).

[Open the map](seattle-stussy.geojson) to see the archive and a second stop on Capitol Hill. **35th North** is a skate shop at **1100 E Pike St**. Its Stüssy collection listed a cuff beanie and a reversible Sherpa jacket on November 21. [See the shop’s Stüssy collection](https://35thnorth.com/collections/stussy).

An old search result can point you to a third address. Capitol Hill Seattle News covered a Stüssy opening at **1108 E Pike** on Black Friday in **2009**. For the 2026 archive, use Bellevue Ave. [Read the 2009 story](https://www.capitolhillseattle.com/2009/11/new-retailer-on-pike-stussy-comes-to-capitol-hill/).

For the separate question of Black Friday discounts, [read within’s Stüssy guide](https://shopwithin.co/black-friday/stussy?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=stussy_seattle_archive_map).

## About the map
